[ How it works ]

One picture of every identity,
built from the systems you already run.

No single system can tell you that HR ended someone's employment while Salesforce still lets them in. AnalyzeSec can, because it reads all of them.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Reads accounts, roles, MFA and sign-ins from HR, Okta, GitHub, AWS and Salesforce.

  2. 2

    Match

    Joins every account to one person. Accounts that match no one are flagged as orphans.

  3. 3

    Detect

    Runs rules for offboarding gaps, weak MFA, dormant admins, unusual access and service accounts.

  4. 4

    Rank

    Scores each finding from 0 to 100 by privilege, system and evidence, so the worst comes first.

  5. 5

    Act

    You fix it, assign it, accept it with a reason, or snooze it. The next sync confirms the change.

Every finding comes with its evidence.

A score tells you where to look. The evidence tells you what to do. Each finding shows exactly which record triggered it.

[ Principles ]

A security tool should hold itself
to its own rules.

Read-only by default

AnalyzeSec reads and recommends. Changing access in your systems will be opt-in, per connector, and always your click.

Least privilege, including ours

It never asks for broader permissions than its own rules would allow any other app to hold.

Every number traces to a record

Click any count on the dashboard and you see the accounts behind it. No figure appears without its source.
